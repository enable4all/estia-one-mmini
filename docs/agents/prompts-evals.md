# What Gemma sees: prompts, context, tools, the brain and the evals

## Layout

- `soul` (the user's soul.md, read for every answer and never written by code: style only, below the rules; its `[chat]` and `[call]` parts, `soul_part`, `has_parts`: `/soul`, doctor and the log show what texts and calls each get)
- `prompts` (every text Gemma or the user sees; her built-in styles, `BUILT_IN_STYLES` (`HEARTH_STYLE`, the default, and `PLAYFUL_STYLE`), their call versions, `BUILT_IN_CALL_STYLES`, `CALM_ON_WORRY` and code's line after a soul.md, `SOUL_CALM`; the evening note's words, `EVENING_NOTE`; the language rule line and each language's spoken call lines; `capabilities`: what she can do in this install, one line and one example per part that is on, with each level's current value (the calendars by service, `CalendarSource.service`, and the lights and plugs by brand, `DeviceSpec.service`, each once), which her rules carry right before Safety (never on a call: `without_capabilities`) and `/help` shows word for word, `help_text`; the evals' harness carries its install's list, `install_list`, and `what_can_you_do_here` and `not_set_up_here` run a smaller install: every part on named, none off, and an off part asked about is "not set up"; `inventory` (prompts/inventory.py; owner, 23 Oct): her tools' list, one plain line per tool offered in this install (`TOOL_LINES`, in the order she gets them; a tool without a line, as another branch adds, gets its own description's first sentence) and the programs behind them that are on (`HELPER_LINES`: the voice service, the call, calendar, device, YouTube and documents helpers; the voice service's line names who speaks her answers from her English voice in use and `[voice] language_voice`, `_SPOKEN`: Pocket TTS, Kokoro, Supertonic-3, and /voice rebuilds her rules so it follows a Kokoro voice; review of 0.9.8, guards-6: it named Kokoro and Supertonic-3 only), built from the live schemas by `StatusHost.inventory` (`Tools.inventory_tools`: mail's tools only with mail, though they are offered without it), which her rules carry right after the capabilities list under `INVENTORY_RULE` (never on a call: `without_capabilities` cuts both lists) and `/tools` shows word for word (`inventory_text`); the evals' harness carries its install's list, `harness_inventory`)
- `context` (Gemma's input; `ContextBuilder.style(call=…)`: the soul.md part in use with code's calm line after it, else the built-in style, on a call its call version)
- `code_notes` (code's notes in her history as code_notes results: the order, the line before each, the limits, `drawn_from_notes`)
- `tools` (Gemma's tools; `Tools.inventory_tools`: what her tools' list is built from)
- `brain` (tool loop)
- `claims` (whether an answer says a reminder or a call is set, in the 11 languages: a precise set for the guard in `brain` and `initiative`'s text check, never an ordinary sentence; a broad, stricter set for the evals' judge and for brain's guard when a reminder was asked, in this message or the one before, or she listed or tried one (`claiming_clauses(…, broad=True)`; release review of 0.9.8, E05); in both, a call or reminder that waits for the user to give the time in its sentence is none, English and Dutch: `_waits_for_your_time`, below, The 0.9.8 release gate; whether it says a light or plug was changed, English first: `claims_a_switch`; whether it promises a lookup it never made, in the 11 languages: `promises_a_lookup`, brain's lookup guard)
