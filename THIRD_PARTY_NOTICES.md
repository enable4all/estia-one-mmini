## Map data

Places and routes from OpenStreetMap use its data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0 (<https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>). Wherever Estia shows those results (her answer's
Sources note), the note credits them in these words: "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors", with that link. A route
from FOSSGIS's servers adds a link to fix the map (<https://www.openstreetmap.org/fixthemap>), as FOSSGIS asks;
public transport from Transitous adds a link to its data sources (<https://transitous.org/sources/>), as Transitous
asks. No result is stored: only her answer and the Sources note, with up to three place names and their links, stay in the chat history. Usage policies followed: Nominatim's
(<https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/>), FOSSGIS's routing servers'
(<https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html>) and Transitous's (<https://transitous.org/api/>): a User-Agent
naming Estia and its site, at most one request a second to each, no bulk or periodic requests, and, for FOSSGIS and
Transitous, non-commercial use only.
